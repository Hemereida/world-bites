📚 4 SIGNATURE DESTINATION MODULES

Master English Through World Cuisine

🇲🇽 MEXICO · A1 DISCOVER

Module 1: Colors & Flavors of Mexico

Step into vibrant Oaxaca markets. Learn foundational English vocabulary for ingredients, spices, quantities, and restaurant conversations.

Learning Outcomes:
  • ✓ Describe textures, aromas, and spice levels accurately.
  • ✓ Order confidently and ask about traditional ingredients.
FINAL PROJECT
Create and present an original bilingual Mexican menu.
🇨🇴 COLOMBIA · A2 PREPARE

Module 2: Hearth & Heritage of Colombia

From Antioquia’s mountains to the Caribbean coast, master action verbs, sequencing words, and kitchen measurements.

Learning Outcomes:
  • ✓ Use imperative verbs and sequence connectors (First, Next, Finally).
  • ✓ Narrate family food memories in clear past simple sentences.
FINAL PROJECT
Record or write a step-by-step Colombian arepa tutorial.
🇯🇵 JAPAN · B1 EXPLORE

Module 3: Precision & Ritual in Japan

Discover Washoku philosophy, seasonal etiquette, and umami craftsmanship while building intermediate fluency.

Learning Outcomes:
  • ✓ Compare dining etiquette and traditions across cultures.
  • ✓ Express opinions, preferences, and cultural respect fluently.
FINAL PROJECT
Deliver a comparative presentation on Japanese vs. local food cultures.
🇫🇷 FRANCE · B2 SHARE

Module 4: Terroir & Discourse in France

Enter Parisian bistros and Lyon’s gastronomic halls. Master nuanced critique, persuasive speaking, and cultural debate.

Learning Outcomes:
  • ✓ Articulate sophisticated culinary critiques and abstract ideas.
  • ✓ Defend viewpoints on sustainability, terroir, and heritage.
FINAL PROJECT
Participate in a B2 roundtable defending cultural food viewpoints.
